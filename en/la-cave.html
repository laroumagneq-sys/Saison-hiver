The Cellar

Wines & pairings

A cellar designed as a service: great names, old vintages and independent winegrowers, chosen to elevate every dish.

The Cellar — 04
01

Exceptional

Old vintages

Petrus, Château La Mission Haut-Brion, Château Latour… the cellar holds a few exceptional bottles, sourced over the years for great occasions. Every bottle is chosen with the same care as the dish it accompanies.

02

Off season

On the road to the winegrowers

Off season, Quentin visits the estates, from the rolled pebbles of Châteauneuf-du-Pape to the forgotten grape varieties of Champagne, by way of Corsica and the French Riviera. These visits uncover rare cuvées, sometimes reserved for those who take the time to come in person.

03

Pairings

Food & wine

A pairing is never improvised: it is built around the dish, the season and the mood of the moment. A characterful red for meat, a taut white for fish, bubbles for the aperitif. And yes, even a sandwich deserves the right glass.

04

Selection

A committed cellar

Châteauneuf-du-Pape, Brunello di Montalcino, Pomerol… renowned labels and lesser-known estates, chosen for their character as much as for their name.

05

Independents

Independent winegrowers

Alongside the classified growths, there is room for independent winegrowers: living wines, made in small quantities, that speak of a terroir and a way of making wine.
