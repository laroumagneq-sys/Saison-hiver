Your evening, in three steps

A simple process, so all you have to do is sit down at the table.

  1. I.

    You get in touch

    Your dates, your chalet, the number of guests and your wishes, on WhatsApp or through the form.

  2. II.

    We create the menu

    Plated or to share, with its wine pairings, allergies and preferences taken into account.

  3. III.

    You host
