Partners

Why entrust us with your clients

What you recommend reflects on your reputation. Here is what you can count on.

  1. i.

    A single point of contact

    Kitchen, table service and wine are all handled by the duo: no need to coordinate several suppliers for one evening.

  2. ii.

    Responsiveness

    A same-day dinner or a whole stay to arrange: message us, we will be there.

  3. iii.

    Discretion

    Nothing is published, photographed or shared without consent. A non-disclosure agreement can be signed on request.

  4. iv.

    Fine-dining standards

    Careers in 2 and 3 Michelin-starred restaurants, a WSET Level 3 sommelier, private butler experience: your clients enjoy the standards they are used to.

  5. v.

    Every format

    A one-off lunch or dinner, even at the last minute, or half board and full board by the week or by the month, in Méribel and Courchevel.

For a quick reply

Send us these details in your first message and we will come back to you with a complete proposal.

  • 01The dates and the chalet (resort, name or address)
  • 02The number of guests, including children
  • 03The format: single meal, half board or full board
  • 04Allergies, dietary requirements and preferences
  • 05The style of service and the standards expected
  • 06Access to the kitchen and its equipment
