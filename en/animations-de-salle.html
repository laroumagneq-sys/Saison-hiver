Table Service

Service as a performance

Carving, flambéing, decanting: a few tableside gestures that turn a dinner into a moment your guests will remember.

Table Service — 03
01

Signature

Carving, in front of your guests

A whole joint brought to the table and carved in front of you: a precise gesture, a shared moment. Nothing is prepared behind the scenes when the show can happen before your eyes.

02

At the guéridon

Precision in every gesture

At the guéridon, every slice is served at the right thickness and the right temperature. Table service as it is done in the finest restaurants.

The tableside repertoire

More gestures to choose from, depending on your menu and the mood of the evening.

  1. 03

    Opening with port tongs

    When the cork crumbles or will not give, red-hot tongs cut the neck cleanly, without a single shard of glass in the wine. A spectacular gesture, inherited from the oldest vintages.

  2. 04

    The sauce, pressed before you

    As with a silver press, the carcass is pressed at the table to extract its juices: a sauce finished before your eyes, in the style of canard au sang, for a dish completed at the table.

  3. 05

    A cloud on your plate

    A fine cloud of helium bubbles settles on the dish as it is served: a moment suspended between poetry and surprise, before the cloud fades away.

  4. 06

    Decanting, with care

    Separating the wine from its sediment and letting it breathe just enough: decanting is a gesture of respect as much as technique, designed to reveal every great bottle.

  5. 07

    Flambéing

    A flame rising above the dessert, the scent of caramel and spirits drifting over the table for a moment: a tableside classic that always lifts the mood.

  6. 08

    Sea bass in a salt crust

    Signature moment: the whole fish arrives in its salt crust, which is broken open and carved before your eyes, for pearly flesh served immediately.

  7. 09

    Dry-aged rib of beef

    Signature moment: the dry-aged rib carved at the table, with its bottle of red wine opened with port tongs. Two gestures for one moment.

  8. 10

    Bresse chicken en vessie

    Signature moment: a great classic of French cuisine, in the style of Paul Bocuse. The chicken, cooked in a bladder, is opened and carved in front of the guests.
